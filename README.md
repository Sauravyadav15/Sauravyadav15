## About Me

I've always been curious about how things work.

It started with taking apart toys and fixing small things, and eventually that curiosity shifted toward computers and technology. What stuck with me is the habit of going to the core of a problem instead of just patching what's broken.

That's probably what I like most about Computer Engineering. I can move between different layers of a problem — hardware, software, data, and the systems connecting them — and understand how the pieces fit together.

I like building things when there's a real problem behind them. **BillSplit** came from two frustrations with existing apps: they took too much time to add expenses, and they confused **equal with fair**. I wanted a faster way to scan a receipt, choose what each person actually used, and pay their fair share — not simply split everything equally.

I'm a third-year Computer Engineering student at Queen's University, exploring **software engineering, backend systems, and machine learning**.

Ultimately, I want to become the kind of engineer who can take a messy real-world problem, turn it into something solvable, and build a useful solution.

## Featured Projects

### 🧾 [BillSplit — Full-Stack Expense Splitting Platform](https://github.com/Sauravyadav15/BillSplitterApp)

> **Equal isn't always fair.**

A receipt-based expense splitting platform designed around **item-level fairness**, rather than forcing everyone into an equal split.

* 📸 Scan receipts and automatically extract line items using OCR
* 👥 Assign individual items to the people who actually used them
* 💰 Calculate each person's share with exact per-item rounding
* 🔐 JWT authentication and secure user flows
* 🗄️ PostgreSQL relational data model with transactional updates
* ☁️ Deployed using Vercel, Render, and Neon
* ⚡ Reduced manual per-item splitting from **5+ minutes to under 30 seconds**
* 📊 **15+ active users** and **25+ bills processed** in production

**Stack:** `React` `Node.js` `Express` `PostgreSQL` `JWT` `Cloudinary` `Google Document AI`

[→ View Repository](https://github.com/Sauravyadav15/BillSplitterApp)
